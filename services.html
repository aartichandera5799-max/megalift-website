10GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before the
first move.

A few practical notes on the proposed MegaLift service model.

Where does MegaLift operate? +

The proposed starting geography is the Western India Gujarat–Maharashtra corridor, with a Vadodara base and proposed hubs at Mundra, Dahej and JNPT. Availability and hub readiness must be confirmed.

What cargo can you move? +

The brochure describes transformers, wind blades and towers, reactors and boilers, bridge girders, metro coaches and mining machinery. Every move needs a cargo and route review.

Are the prices fixed? +

No. The listed ranges are indicative proposal prices excluding GST. Actual pricing depends on dimensions, weight, route, permits, escort, handling, equipment and site conditions.

Is MegaLift Track live today? +

The website shows an illustrative concept. Live tracking, milestone alerts and customer access depend on the operational systems and equipment available for a specific move.

Are the delivery guarantees available? +

The brochure proposes on-time and damage-free guarantees. They are not represented here as active contractual terms; eligibility, exclusions, remedy and service readiness would need to be agreed in writing.

What information helps scope a move? +

Share cargo weight and dimensions, centre of gravity if known, origin and destination, timing, loading/unloading requirements, cargo value and any route or site constraints.
